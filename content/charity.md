This year's donations from OpoCon will go to "Mausohr Bahnhof". You can make a donation directly via OpoCon’s fundraising campaign on [betterplace.com](https://opocon.gay/charity).


![<Mausohr Bahnhof>](/img/mobf_tit.png)

**Mausohr Bahnhof** is an old railway station located in Mümling-Grumbach, about 30 minutes from OpoCon. The station has been in existence since 1895 and has been home to bats since 1930. After the former owner went bankrupt, the station was taken over by SPA® & HGON in 2012. 

They are committed to preserving the station for the bats and turning it into an information centre for visitors to enjoy. So lets walk among with us on this charity event and help the bats to keep their home and safe the fragile environment.

More information under [Mausohr Bahnhof Website](https://mausohr-bahnhof.muemling-grumbach.de/).
