Here are just a few of the activities you’ll be able to experience at OpoCon. 

**Don't hesitate to suggest your own events to the staff, because OpoCon thrives on shared experiences.**

- Open & Closing Ceremony
- Karaoke & Cheese & Wine Tasting
- Pen & Paper Adventures
- Miniature Figure Painting Workshop
- Marmalade Unplugged
- Live DJs & Dances
- VR Portal
- Scavenger Hunt
- Sticker Trade
- Live Streams

and more...
